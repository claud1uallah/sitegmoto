## Hero generat pentru GTune

`hero-v2.png` este un vizual original generat cu instrumentul built-in ImageGen, pentru acest proiect. Este o imagine generată, nu o fotografie a unui vehicul real. Imaginea este destinată ilustrației editoriale și nu indică asocierea sau afilierea GTune cu un producător auto.

Promptul final folosit:

> Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: premium automotive ecommerce website hero background. Primary request: Photorealistic cinematic automotive photograph, panoramic 16:9 composition, of a graphite black and silver Porsche 911-style sports coupe on a smooth mountain road at blue-hour dusk. A single sports car in front three-quarter view facing the left, positioned in the RIGHT HALF of the frame with the whole car visible and large. The left 45 percent must be mostly dark empty asphalt and dark atmospheric scenery suitable for white web headline overlay. A low dramatic camera angle, subtle motion blur in the background, headlights glowing pure white, polished dark metallic bodywork, refined pale blue reflections, modern black wheels, misty mountain ridges receding in the distance, almost-black cool navy sky. The car is the distinct hero subject with realistic proportions and crisp body detail. Luxury editorial car commercial photography, sophisticated black/slate palette and subtle cool sky-blue lighting, natural realistic textures. No typography, no text, no lettering, no website UI, no collage, no watermark, no added logo. Wide horizontal crop for a website banner.
